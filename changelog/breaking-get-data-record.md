- `get_data(year, month, *, commodity=None, region=None, table=None)` answers one `WasdeRecord` per value of a month's tables, complete in itself: the report's number and month, the table, the commodity, the region, the market year with its status, the forecast month, the row label with its class and statistic, the unit, `value` as printed with `value_number` beside it, and the footnotes with their texts, each label beside its `_label`, and no page or matrix. It replaces the bulk `get_release` and its `WasdeXmlCell`, whose `cell_value` is now `value` and whose number is now `value_number`, and its filters compare the records' `_label` fields after the fetch. On pages 12 to 15 the commodity is the block title of the same release's XLS, else a fixed table of the report's layout, and `commodity_source` says `title`, `xls` or `layout`. (ADR-0060, https://github.com/xippios/sdk-usda-wasde/issues/57)
